Grammar Rule Recap / Resumen Gramatical

WILL

• Spontaneous decisions: "I will move to the side!"

• Instant emergency reactions: "I'll run!"

(Decisiones espontáneas y reacciones inmediatas al momento)

AM GOING TO

• Planned future actions: "I am going to bring an umbrella."

• Intentions set in advance: "I am going to bring money next time!"

(Planes futuros e intenciones decididas con anticipación)
